# Studio Owner Guide

Running your studio's whole business from one place.

## Getting started

1. Sign in to Studio Console with the credentials from your welcome
   email.
2. Complete your studio's own setup: logo and brand colors, your
   team, your service catalog and packages.
3. Invite your team — see [Team Guide](/docs/team-guide) for what
   they can do once invited.

[Screenshot: Studio onboarding wizard, step 1 of 5]

## Your first lead to your first payment

1. **Capture the lead** — add it manually, or connect an
   [enquiry form](/docs/enquiry-form-guide) so leads come in
   automatically from your website.
2. **Send a quotation** — see [Quotation Guide](/docs/quotation-guide).
3. **Confirm the booking** once the client accepts.
4. **Schedule the event** — assign team, equipment, and a shot list.
5. **Deliver the gallery** — see [Gallery Guide](/docs/gallery-guide).
6. **Get paid** — see [Billing Guide](/docs/billing-guide).

## Day-to-day tools

- **Dashboard** — today's events, recent leads, and outstanding
  invoices at a glance.
- **Copilot** — ask it things in plain language: "Show tomorrow's
  events," "Which clients haven't selected their album photos yet?"
- **Automation** — set up rules that run themselves, like a WhatsApp
  reminder when a payment is overdue.
- **Marketing Studio** — generate Instagram captions, reel scripts,
  and festival greetings in your studio's own voice.
- **Reports** — 22 different reports covering leads, revenue,
  profitability, team performance, and more.

## Branches and team roles

If you run more than one location, add each as a **Branch** and
assign team members accordingly. Roles and permissions control who
can see and do what — see [Team Guide](/docs/team-guide).

## Your own custom domain

Want your gallery links to read `gallery.yourstudio.com` instead of
the platform's own domain? See [Custom Domain Setup](/docs/custom-domain-setup).

## Getting help

If something isn't working the way you expect, check
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) first — most common issues
are covered there.
