# Client Guide

For clients of a studio using BCP Studio OS — here's how to view your
gallery, select your photos, sign contracts, and pay your invoices.

## Signing in to your Client Portal

Your studio sends you a link and a one-time code by email or WhatsApp
— there's no password to remember.

1. Open the link your studio sent you.
2. Enter the code sent to your email or phone.
3. You're in — you'll see everything tied to you: your bookings,
   galleries, contracts, and invoices.

[Screenshot: Client Portal OTP sign-in screen]

## Viewing your gallery

Your studio will send you a direct gallery link once your photos are
ready — you don't need to sign in to the full portal just to view and
download your gallery, unless your studio has set it to require sign-in.

[Screenshot: Client gallery grid view]

## Finding yourself with face search

If your studio has this feature turned on, you can take a selfie
right in the gallery to instantly find every photo you're in — your
selfie is never saved, it's only used for that one search.

[Screenshot: "Find my photos" face search flow]

## Selecting photos for your album

1. Open your album's selection link.
2. Browse your gallery and tap the photos you want.
3. Favorite your top picks if you're undecided.
4. Once you're happy with your selection, tap **Submit** — this locks
   your selection in for your studio to start designing your album.

Changed your mind after submitting? Ask your studio to reopen your
selection — they're the only ones who can.

[Screenshot: Photo selection screen with a photo enlarged and a heart/select icon]

## Signing a contract

1. Open the contract link your studio sent you.
2. Review it, then continue to sign.
3. Enter the one-time code sent to confirm it's really you.
4. Type your name to sign.

You'll get a signed copy automatically once it's complete.

## Paying an invoice

From your portal's **Invoices** tab, you can view what you owe and
either pay online (if your studio has this enabled) or mark that
you've paid another way, which your studio will confirm on their end.

[Screenshot: Client invoice view with a Pay Now button]
