# Enquiry Form Guide

Turn your website visitors into leads automatically.

## Creating a form

1. Go to **Enquiry Forms** → **New Form**.
2. Choose which fields to include — name, phone, event type, date,
   budget, and any custom questions you want to ask.
3. Save the form to get an embed code and a direct link.

[Screenshot: Enquiry form builder with drag-and-drop fields]

## Adding it to your website

Paste the embed code into your website, or simply link to the direct
form URL from your Instagram bio or ad campaigns.

## What happens when someone submits

Every submission becomes a new **Lead** automatically, tagged with
the source form so you always know which of your marketing channels
is actually working. You can set up an automation to send an instant
WhatsApp or email reply the moment someone submits, so no enquiry
waits until you happen to check your phone.

[Screenshot: New lead notification, showing the enquiry form as its source]

## Spam protection

Every form includes built-in spam protection — you won't need to
manually filter out bot submissions.

## Reviewing your sources

The **Sources** report shows exactly which forms (and other lead
sources) are bringing in the most enquiries and, more importantly,
the most *converted* bookings — not just the most form submissions.
