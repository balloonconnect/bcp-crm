# Billing Guide

Invoicing, payments, and keeping track of what your studio is owed.

## Creating an invoice

1. From a confirmed booking, choose **New Invoice** — or generate one
   directly from an accepted quotation, which carries the amounts
   over automatically.
2. Review the line items, tax, and any advance payment already
   collected.
3. Send it to your client by email, WhatsApp, or a direct link.

[Screenshot: Invoice creation screen with tax breakdown]

## Recording a payment

- **Online payments** — if you've connected a payment gateway (see
  [Payment Setup](/docs/payment-setup)), your client can pay directly
  from their invoice link, and it's recorded automatically.
- **Manual payments** — cash, bank transfer, or anything else — record
  it yourself with the amount, method, and date.

[Screenshot: Record payment form]

## Outstanding amounts

The **Outstanding Amounts** report shows every unpaid or partially
paid invoice across your studio, so nothing quietly falls through the
cracks. Set up an automation to send a WhatsApp reminder automatically
once an invoice passes its due date.

## Receipts

A receipt is generated automatically the moment a payment is recorded
— your client receives it without you needing to create it by hand.

## Understanding your profit

The **Profit** report ties an event's actual revenue against its
recorded expenses (freelancer payments, equipment, travel, and more)
so you can see which kinds of events are genuinely worth your time,
not just which ones bring in the most revenue.
