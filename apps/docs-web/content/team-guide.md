# Team Guide

For photographers, editors, and coordinators working inside a studio.

## Accepting your invitation

1. Open the invitation email from your studio and click **Accept
   Invitation**.
2. Set your own password (or sign in with Google, if your studio has
   that enabled).
3. Turn on two-factor authentication — recommended for every account.

[Screenshot: Accept invitation screen]

## What you can see and do

Your studio owner assigns you a role, which decides what you can
access — you might only see events you're assigned to, or you might
have full access to clients and billing. If something you need is
missing, ask your studio owner to review your role's permissions.

## Your day

- **My Schedule** — the events you're assigned to, shot lists, and
  equipment checklists for each.
- **Tasks** — anything assigned to you, with due dates.
- **Attendance** — clock in and out for shoots you're working.

[Screenshot: Team member's daily schedule view]

## Working offline

If you're at a venue with poor signal, the app keeps working for the
essentials — viewing your schedule, contacts, and shot lists, adding
notes, and checking off equipment — and syncs automatically the
moment you're back online. Anything that needs the studio's
authorization right away (like sending an invoice) will simply wait
until you're connected, rather than pretending to have gone through.

## Editing and delivering photos

Once you've uploaded photos to a gallery, the platform automatically
generates web-friendly versions and thumbnails, and can run AI photo
analysis to flag blurry shots, near-duplicates, and suggest your best
shots — a starting point for culling, not a replacement for your own
eye.
