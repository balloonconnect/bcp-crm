# Gallery Guide

Creating and delivering photo galleries to your clients.

## Creating a gallery

1. From an event, go to **Galleries** → **New Gallery**.
2. Give it a title and choose which event it's linked to.
3. Set access — public link, password-protected, or sign-in required.
4. Choose whether to allow downloads, and whether to enable face
   search for this gallery.

[Screenshot: New gallery creation form]

## Uploading photos

1. Open the gallery and choose **Upload Photos**.
2. Drag in your files, or select a folder — large batches upload in
   the background so you can keep working.
3. The platform automatically generates a web-optimized version and
   thumbnail for every photo.

[Screenshot: Bulk upload in progress with a progress bar]

## AI photo intelligence

Once photos are uploaded, you can run AI analysis to flag blurry
shots, detect near-duplicates, and get culling suggestions weighted
toward your priorities (emotional moments protected, repeated poses
deprioritized). This is a starting point for your own review, never
an automatic deletion — nothing is ever removed without you approving
it.

[Screenshot: AI photo intelligence panel with quality scores]

## Face search

Turn this on per gallery so your clients can find their own photos by
taking a selfie. It requires their explicit consent each time, and
their selfie is discarded immediately after the search — never stored.

## Sharing the gallery

Copy the gallery link and send it however you like — email, WhatsApp,
or your own channels. A password-protected gallery also needs you to
share the password separately.

## Watermarks

If you want to protect preview-quality images before a client pays in
full, turn on watermarking for the gallery — the final delivered
versions can be watermark-free once you're ready.
