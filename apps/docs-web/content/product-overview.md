# Product Overview

BCP Studio OS is a complete operating system for photography studios —
from the first enquiry through delivery, payment, and everything a
studio and its clients need in between.

## What it does

- **Sales**: lead capture, enquiry forms, quotations, bookings.
- **Production**: event scheduling, shot lists, equipment checklists,
  team scheduling, task workflows.
- **Delivery**: photo galleries with AI-powered face search, client
  photo selection, album design, e-albums, contracts and e-signature.
- **Money**: invoicing, payments, expenses, profit tracking, print
  product commerce.
- **Growth**: an AI marketing studio for social content, an AI Studio
  Copilot for day-to-day questions, and automation for repetitive
  studio workflows.
- **Platform**: one platform serving many studios, each fully
  isolated from the others, with its own branding, team, clients, and
  (optionally) its own custom domain.

## Who uses it

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Platform Admin** | Operates the platform itself — onboards studios, manages subscriptions and licenses, monitors system health |
| **Studio Owner** | Runs their own studio's whole business inside the platform |
| **Team Member** | Photographers, editors, and coordinators working a studio's events |
| **Client** | The studio's own customers — view their gallery, select photos, sign contracts, pay invoices |

See the guide for your own role: [Platform Admin Guide](/docs/platform-admin-guide),
[Studio Owner Guide](/docs/studio-owner-guide), [Team Guide](/docs/team-guide),
or [Client Guide](/docs/client-guide).

## How the product is organized

BCP Studio OS is several applications working together, not one
monolith:

- **Studio Console** — the day-to-day app a studio's own team uses.
- **Client Portal** — where a studio's clients log in.
- **Gallery** — the public-facing photo gallery and selection
  experience.
- **Platform Admin** — the operator's own control panel.
- **Marketing site, docs site, status page** — public-facing.

See [Architecture](/docs/architecture) for how these fit together
technically.
