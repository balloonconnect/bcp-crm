# Backup and Restore

How your studio's data is protected, and how to request a backup or
restore.

## Requesting a backup

1. From Studio Console, go to **Settings → Data Tools → Backups**.
2. Choose **Request Backup**.
3. Your request is sent to your platform's own admin for approval —
   this is intentional: a full studio backup is sensitive, and
   creating one always requires a second person's sign-off, not
   something a single account can trigger alone.

[Screenshot: Backup request screen showing a "Pending Approval" status]

## Once approved

Your backup is generated and encrypted — nobody, including platform
staff, can read its contents without the encryption key, which is
managed entirely separately from the backup file itself.

## Requesting a restore

Restoring works the same way — request it, a platform admin approves
it, and the process begins. Because restoring can affect live data,
this same two-person approval applies here too, with no exceptions.

## What gets backed up

Your studio's core business data — clients, leads, events, bookings,
quotations, invoices, galleries, and albums. Your own exports (see
below) are the way to get a complete, immediately-usable copy of
everything, including photo files themselves.

## Exporting your own data any time

You don't need anyone's approval to export your own studio's data —
from **Settings → Data Tools → Export**, choose what you want (leads,
clients, invoices, or everything) and download it as a CSV or,
for a complete copy, a single bundled export.

[Screenshot: Data export screen with export-type checkboxes]

## If you need to delete your account or your studio

See your platform admin — account and studio deletion both follow a
deliberate, careful process rather than an instant, irreversible
button, precisely because they're not something you want to happen by
accident.
